"Kids Fly High, Parents Guide the Way."

Bé Ong is a delightful, privacy-first family chore and habit tracking app designed to help children build healthy daily routines, foster independence, and learn essential financial literacy through hands-on positive reinforcement.

KEY FEATURES:

1. BUILD DAILY HABITS & INDEPENDENCE
• 20+ age-appropriate chore presets designed for children ages 3 and up.
• Create structured routines for Morning, After School, and Bedtime.
• Kids actively complete tasks and earn honey coins with pride.

2. FINANCIAL LITERACY WITH 3 SAVING JARS
• Encourage kids to distribute their earned coins into 3 jars: Save, Spend, and Give.
• Set goals for desired toys, books, or activities and watch savings grow.
• Teach the value of effort and thoughtful budgeting early in life.

3. 100% TRANSPARENT LEDGER
• An append-only transaction history records every coin earned or spent with full details.
• No silent score changes — building lasting trust between parents and children.

4. PARENTAL COMPANIONSHIP & APPROVAL
• Flexible review modes: Automatic or Parent Approved with optional photo evidence.
• Send encouraging praise messages when approving completed tasks.
• Beautiful stats and insights to understand your child's growth without pressure.

5. ZERO ADS, ZERO TRACKING, 100% PRIVATE
• Works fully offline on your device.
• NO advertisements. NO in-app purchases.
• NO third-party trackers or child data collection.
• Individual profile PIN protection and fast QR code pairing between devices.
